          A note on the source

          This palette follows slides 5 and 7 of the 2026 Canva guideline. RGB values for Warm Muted and Soft Gold are corrected from their HEX codes. "Moka" is named Mocha, and the Burgundy spelling is standardized.

          Imbue and Kufam are bundled with their open font licenses. Finland Rounded needs a separate web license. The club's vision, mission, and production logo files should be finalized separately from this color system.
